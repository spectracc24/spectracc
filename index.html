How we work

Three commitments in every collaboration

We bring care, clarity, and consistency to every collaboration, adapting our approach to each project’s needs.

Evidence-led

We combine reliable information, sound methods, and clear documentation to deliver work partners can understand and use.

Geospatial-focused

We use GIS, remote sensing, and spatial data to make changing landscapes easier to understand.

Sustainability-oriented

Our services and learning programmes help partners make informed choices and support lasting environmental outcomes.

Services

Practical support, applied studies and training

Three practice areas work together so that every partner can grow toward independent technical capacity.

Geospatial Analysis and Technical Support

Geospatial tools and guidance shaped around your project.

  • Geospatial data engineering. QA/QC protocols and spatial database architecture.
  • Spatial decision support. Multi-criteria analysis and decision-support systems.
  • Remote sensing. Optical, SAR and high-resolution imagery processing.
  • Spatial modeling and machine learning. Prediction, classification and data fusion.
  • Carbon and climate analytics. Carbon stock, emissions baselines and climate risk.
  • Cartography and visualization. Publication-grade maps and atlases.

Applied Studies and Assessments

Practical assessments that help clarify project conditions, options, and progress.

  • Environmental baseline assessments. Reference conditions and landscape diagnostics.
  • Technical frameworks and protocols. Methods, assessment frameworks and guidelines.
  • Feasibility and readiness assessment. Project viability and implementation readiness.
  • Monitoring, evaluation and performance. Indicators, tracking and impact evaluation.
  • Applied environmental research. Building a clearer understanding of environmental conditions and change.

Training and Institutional Capacity Development

Competency-based programs tailored to each team and its workflows.

  • Applied GIS and cartography. Spatial analysis and map production.
  • Remote sensing and Earth observation. Forest cover mapping and land-cover change.
  • Geospatial databases and field data. Data organization, collection protocols and QC/QA.

About Us

Geospatial expertise for environmental progress.

Spectra Citra Cendekia brings together multidisciplinary expertise to help environmental initiatives understand places, make informed decisions, and strengthen their technical capabilities.

Who we are

Foundations first, independence next

Spectra CC focuses on early and mid-stage environmental projects and growing organizations. We establish strong geospatial capabilities and practical systems that help these initiatives grow independently and contribute to lasting environmental outcomes.

Our work focuses on geospatial applications for environmental sustainability, particularly across Agriculture, Forestry, and Other Land Use (AFOLU).

Our vision

Become a trusted partner for geospatial insight, practical innovation, and environmental sustainability.

Mission

What we commit to

Delivering dependable geospatial work

Providing reliable geospatial analysis and practical technical solutions that support environmental initiatives.

Sharing knowledge and building capabilities

Practical studies, accessible training, and knowledge sharing—alongside useful workflows and spatial data systems that partner teams can manage.

Empowering independent environmental leadership

Stronger technical foundations and organizational capability, so institutions and emerging practitioners can run credible, independent and sustainable initiatives.

Who we serve

Where we choose to work

Established institutions

  • Mature geospatial infrastructure
  • Standardized analytical frameworks
  • Dedicated in-house GIS personnel

Early and mid-stage projects (our priority)

  • High sustainability potential
  • Limited geospatial tools and technical support
  • Few technical staff to scale credibly

Contact

Let’s talk about your project.

Whether you are starting out or ready to grow, send us a short email and we will help identify the next practical step.

Before you write

A short brief helps us reply with something useful.

There is no form to fill in. Just cover these four points in your email.

  1. 1

    Who you are

    Your name, organization and role in the project.

  2. 2

    Your site and goals

    Where the project is located and what you want to achieve.

  3. 3

    Services of interest

    Geospatial analysis and technical support, applied studies, or training.

  4. 4

    Stage and timing

    Early, mid-stage or growing, and any deadline we should know about.
